# RSD Rep Dashboard

Single-page dashboard for the RSD Events Team (CUTCO Rising Sun Division).
Four tabs: **Total** (one season against the earlier years you pick, Event Analyzer layout), **Campaigns** (one campaign compared across years, plus a full-year leaderboard), **Products** (the home office's product report: nine lines, three years, a rep leaderboard per line), **Rep View** (year or campaign, every campaign in one grid, a plan for next year, plus the rep's product mix).

Every core rep also has a **link of their own**: the dashboard address plus their name, for example
`https://allinalan.github.io/rsd-events-dashboard/matt-foss`. It opens their Rep View with no tabs and no name
picker. See *Personal links* below.

**Live URL:** https://allinalan.github.io/rsd-events-dashboard/ (the team page; each rep: that address plus their name)

## Files in this repo

- `index.html` — the dashboard itself (single-file, inline CSS + JS, no build step)
- `data.js` — the data (regenerated by the parser each campaign close)
- `<first>-<last>/index.html` — one small page per core rep, their personal link (written by `tools/rep-pages.mjs`; holds no numbers)
- `404.html` — passes a typed name (`/luke`, a typo) on to the page, which finds the rep or shows the names to pick from
- `apple-touch-icon.png` — the sun mark, the icon Add to Home Screen uses
- `tools/smoke.mjs`, `tools/rep-pages.mjs` — the smoke check and the personal-page writer
- `leader_walkthrough.md` — context doc for events leadership
- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-personal-dashboard-links-design.md` — what the personal links are and why

## Updating data after each campaign

`data.js` is regenerated by the intake on the Mac mini (private repo `allinalan/rsd-events-intake`,
launchd `com.allinalan.rsd-intake`, Jan/May/Sep 8). It opens a PR here; merging it publishes.
`index.html` is edited by hand and never touched by the intake.
Every PR (the intake's too) and every push to main runs the **Dashboard smoke check** (`.github/workflows/smoke.yml`, `tools/smoke.mjs`): it opens each tab at desktop and phone size, then every rep's own link on a phone, and goes red on a console error, the load-error message, an empty Total leaderboard, a frozen page, a core rep without a page (or a page for someone off the team), or a link that opens anyone but that rep. Its `dashboard-screenshots` artifact shows what will publish, every rep's page included; don't merge a red one.

## Personal links

Decided with Alan on 2026-10-18; the design is in `docs/superpowers/specs/`. The links are for convenience,
not privacy: names are the links, and anyone can open anyone's page ("I encourage it even").

- **The link:** `https://allinalan.github.io/rsd-events-dashboard/<first>-<last>`, lower case. GitHub Pages serves
  it from that folder's `index.html`, which sends the browser to `?rep=<first>-<last>`. `index.html` matches the
  full name, then the name without hyphens, then a first or last name only one rep has. Anything else shows the
  names to pick from (the typed text goes in as text, never HTML).
- **A rep's page** (`?rep=`): no tabs, their name in place of the picker, "My numbers" on the scorecard, a link
  back to the team page at the foot. The page title and `apple-mobile-web-app-title` are the rep's name, so Add to
  Home Screen saves their own page under their name. On a phone, a one-time tip says how.
- **The team page** shows the selected rep's link in Rep View with a **Copy link** button.
- **Print every link:** `node tools/rep-pages.mjs --list`.
- **When the core team changes** (`roster.json` in the intake, so `meta.master_list` in `data.js`): run
  `node tools/rep-pages.mjs`. It writes a page for each new rep and takes down the pages of reps who left. Until it
  runs, the smoke check stays red and says who is missing. A rep who left gets the name list, never anyone's numbers.
- **Every campaign** (Rep View, everyone's): C1 / C2 / C3 / Year by year, for Total CPO, Event CPO, Orders, Avg
  order or Events, each figure with its change against the same campaign the year before. Tapping a figure opens
  that campaign or year at the top.
- **Plan next year** (Rep View, everyone's): shows from the day C2 closes until next year's C1 closes. The goal is
  prefilled with this year's pace + 10% (rounded to $5K), split by the rep's own C1 / C2 / C3 mix (skipping any year
  where one campaign is under 5% of it; event sales, then the team's split, when that leaves nothing), with their
  best of each campaign and about how many shifts each takes. The goal is saved only in that browser (`localStorage`).

## Architecture

- 7-bucket taxonomy: Event (Traditional), Service Event, Industry Event, Realtor, Mall, Federal, Non-Event CPO
- Total = a year (closed year: year-end Total Sales; open year: C1 onward); Campaigns = one campaign vs the same campaign in other years
- Products is annual and comes from a separate report (`meta.products`, `reps[name].products`); it compares with the year before unless Compare to picks another. Its tables show the change as +/- units and % change in CPO (a year to date against a full year until the year closes, and labeled so); the cards and headline show the year to date as a share of the compare year
- Event sales exist for every campaign; Total / Non-Event only where a Total Sales workbook (or the year-end figure) exists — never shown as $0
- Annual scope for shifts and pacing baseline
- Per-rep empirical pacing multipliers, capped to [×2.0, ×4.5]
- Opportunity engine surfaces the biggest dollar-uplift improvement per rep

See `leader_walkthrough.md` for the full breakdown.

## Design rules

Set by the Apple HIG pass (PRs #13 and #14, October 2026). `index.html` is edited by hand, often by
different sessions, so these are the rules that keep it one design. Reps read it on phones first.

**Type**
- Two families. Archivo (`--disp`) is for the wordmark and headings only. Everything else, numbers
  included, uses the system font (`--ui`, SF Pro on Apple devices). No monospace.
- Six sizes: 48 hero · 28 title · 20 headline · 15 body · 13 footnote · 11 caption (on phones: hero 40,
  title 24). Nothing under 11px except icon glyphs (the sort arrow, the "i"). 16px appears only on
  selects on touch screens, because anything smaller makes iOS zoom the page when a select is tapped.
- Three weights: 400, 600, 700. 800 belongs to the wordmark alone.
- Letter-spacing tightens only from 20px up (−0.012em headline, −0.02em title, −0.025em hero);
  smaller text stays at 0. No uppercase labels; the wordmark is the one exception.
- Columns of numbers line up through `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on `body`.
- One heading per section. No small label stacked on top of a title.

**Color**
- Every color is a token in `:root` with a dark value in the `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block. No
  hex values in rules or in the script; chart colors are tokens too.
- Text is `--label`, the masthead is `--chrome` / `--chrome-glass`, and a chosen pill or a switch
  that is on is `--selected`. They are separate tokens because they flip differently in dark mode.
- Secondary text (`--ink-soft`, `--ink-faint`) passes 4.5:1 on cards and on the page in both modes.
  Check the contrast of any new text color before using it.

**Surfaces**
- Cards are white on the bone ground with no border. Space separates sections (48px), not lines.
  Hairlines stay only between rows of the number tables, where they guide the eye across columns.
- No decorative accent stripes. Selection is a tint or a single ring.
- The masthead is translucent glass whose brand row scrolls away (`--brand-h`, set by the script).
  `prefers-reduced-transparency` makes it solid.

**Motion**
- One curve: `--spring` (critically damped, settles in `--spring-ms`, 500ms, no overshoot), with a
  cubic-bezier fallback where CSS `linear()` is unsupported. No bounce: nothing here is flicked or dragged.
- Respond on press, not release: `:active` scales to 0.96 in 80ms and springs back.
- Things move from where they are. Leaderboards re-render through `morph()`, so rows slide to their
  new rank and bars change length in place. Single-choice pill groups get one sliding thumb
  (`syncThumb`), and the tab underline slides (`syncTabUnderline`). Entrance animations play once per tab.
- Animate `transform` and `opacity`; never move things with `left` or `top`.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off travel and growing bars and keeps fades and color changes.
- In a MutationObserver callback, use `classList.toggle(name, force)`. `add` and `remove` rewrite the
  attribute even when nothing changes, which wakes the observer again. That loop once froze the page.

**Touch and focus**
- Every control is at least 44×44pt on touch screens (`@media (pointer: coarse)`). Controls that
  look small, like the pills and the "i", get an invisible larger hit area through `::after`.
- An explanation a rep needs goes in the tap-to-open popover (`#tip`, via `data-tip`), not in
  `title=`. A `title=` tooltip never appears on a phone.
- Keyboard focus is always visible (the ember `:focus-visible` ring). Never `outline: none` without
  a replacement.

**Before merging a change to `index.html`:** open all four tabs at 1280px and 375px, in light and
dark mode, plus one rep's own link (`?rep=matt-foss`), and confirm there is no sideways scroll and no console error.
